Read the client, make the right call before the clock runs out, and take the deal from the first meeting all the way to closing day.

## 🎮 About the Game

You are the **listing agent**. Clients, buyers, and other agents talk to you, and you choose how to answer.

Every answer changes how the deal goes. Good answers build trust and move the deal forward. Weak answers cost you time, money, or the client.

The same three homes follow you through the whole career:

| Seller | Home | What makes them different |
|---|---|---|
| 👵 **Linda Park** | 3-bedroom house | Downsizing after 30 years. Emotional about the move. |
| 👨‍💼 **David Chen** | 2-bedroom condo | Investor. Loves numbers. A tenant still lives there. |
| 👩‍💻 **Priya Patel** | 4-bedroom house | Moving out of state for work. She and her husband don't agree on timing. |

The best answer can change from one seller to another, just like real life.

---

## 🗺️ Scenes

The game follows a real sale, step by step. Each scene plays a little differently.

| Scene | Your job | How you win |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · 🤝 **Listing Appointment** | Win the listing from the seller | Keep the seller's **trust** high enough to get a signature |
| 2 · 🏠 **Open House** | Host visitors and earn leads | Collect enough **leads** while protecting your **reputation** and the seller's privacy |
| 3 · 💬 **Negotiation** | Work with buyer agents on offer day | Push the **price** to the seller's goal without letting **deal stability** fall too low |
| 4 · 🔑 **Escrow Rescue** | Keep the deal alive until closing | Solve problems fast and **close on time** with the deal still healthy |

Win a scene on **Normal** to unlock its **Hard** mode and the next scene.

## 🎯 Goal

Practice real conversations before they happen in real life.

- Learn what to say to sellers, buyers, and other agents
- Learn key ideas like pricing, disclosures, offers, contingencies, and escrow
- Learn the mistakes that can cost an agent a deal or a license

Good for new agents, team training, and real estate students.

---

## 📏 Rules

- Each question has **4 answers**. Each one is graded **Best**, **Okay**, **Weak**, or **Bad**.
- Answers are drawn from a larger pool, so the wording changes every game.
- If time runs out before you answer, you lose points.
- ⛔ **Deal-breakers:** Some answers break the rules, like Fair Housing violations, hiding a known problem, sharing a client's private information, or falling for wire fraud. Pick one and the scene ends right away.
- After each answer, a coach explains the best reply and why.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Click **Start** and enter your agent name.
2. Choose a scene on the career map.
3. Choose a difficulty, then a seller or a home.
4. Read the case briefing, then start.
5. Read what the person says and pick your reply.
6. At the end, see your result, your stars, and the coach's review.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1` `2` `3` `4` | Pick an answer |
| `Enter` | Next question / start |
| `Esc` | Pause |

## ❓ FAQ

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It runs in any modern web browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.

**Does it work on a phone?**
Yes. It also works well on a computer or tablet.

**Is my progress saved?**
Yes, in your own browser. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, it starts over.

**Can a team share one computer?**
Yes. Use **New game** or **Switch agent** to create separate agents. Each agent keeps their own stars, cards, and stats.

**Can I unlock everything for training?**
Yes. Go to **Settings** and turn on **Training mode**.

**Can I reset my progress?**
Yes. Go to **Settings** and choose **Reset progress**.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. The game uses simplified examples based on common California practice. Laws, forms, and local rules change. Always check with your broker and the current rules.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free to play.
