## 🌟 Overview

You are the **listing agent**. Sellers, buyers, and other agents talk to you, and you choose how to answer. Every answer changes the deal. Good answers build trust and move the sale forward. Weak answers cost you time, money, or the client.

The game follows one real sale, step by step, from the first meeting with the seller all the way to closing day.

> 💡 Free to play in your browser. No install, no sign-up.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **4 scenes, one career:** listing appointment, open house, negotiation, and escrow
- 👥 **3 sellers with different personalities:** the best answer can change from one seller to another
- 🎯 **Graded answers:** every reply is Best, Okay, Weak, or Bad, and the wording changes every game
- ⛔ **Deal-breakers:** illegal or dishonest answers end the scene on the spot
- 🧑‍🏫 **Coach's notes:** after each answer, see the best reply and why it works
- 📚 **56 knowledge cards:** unlock real topics like CMA, dual agency, escalation clauses, and wire fraud
- 🏆 **Stars, ranks, and certificates:** climb from Rookie to Legend
- 🎆 **A special career finale** when you finish all four scenes
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Team friendly:** several agents can share one computer, and Training mode unlocks everything

## 🗺️ Scenes

| Scene | Your job | How you win |
|---|---|---|
| 🤝 **1 · Listing Appointment** | Win the listing from the seller | Keep the seller's **trust** high enough to get a signature |
| 🏠 **2 · Open House** | Host visitors and earn leads | Collect enough **leads** and protect your **reputation** and the seller's privacy |
| 💬 **3 · Negotiation** | Handle offers from buyer agents | Reach the seller's **price** goal without letting **deal stability** fall too low |
| 🔑 **4 · Escrow Rescue** | Keep the deal alive until closing | Solve problems fast and **close on time** with a healthy deal |

Win a scene on **Normal** to unlock its **Hard** mode and the next scene.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Click **Start** and enter your agent name
2. Choose a scene on the **career map**
3. Choose a difficulty, then a seller or a home
4. Read the case briefing and start
5. Read what the person says and pick one of **4 replies**
6. At the end, see your result, your stars, and the coach's review

---

## 📏 Rules

- ✅ **Best** answers help the most. **Okay** answers help a little.
- ❌ **Weak** and **Bad** answers hurt the deal.
- ⏱️ If time runs out before you answer, you lose points.
- ⛔ **Deal-breakers** end the scene right away, for example:
  - breaking Fair Housing rules
  - hiding a known problem with the home
  - sharing a client's private information
  - falling for wire fraud
- 🏁 Each scene has its own win goal: trust, leads, price, or closing on time.
